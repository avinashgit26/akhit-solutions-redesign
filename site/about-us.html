Our History

In 2020, Kugan, the visionary founder of AKH IT Solutions, boarded the talented Subangan to lead the company as the Director. And then assembled a team of like-minded professionals, all dedicated to providing the best possible services to clients.

During the face of unprecedented challenges, Subangan and his team embraced the Work From Home model and continue to deliver top-quality services without compromising efficiency or productivity.

As AKH's client list continues to grow, Kugan expands the team with even more skilled and dedicated employees, ensuring that all clients receive the best support and service. Through his leadership and innovative approach, Kugan has consistently helped AKH adapt and thrive, building a company that is poised for continued success in the rapidly changing world of IT solutions.
